My Learning Resources:

The following are the learning resources i use in my frontend journey:

YouTube: Tutorial based learning.

YouTube is where i watch tutorials on frontend development and observe how technologies are demonstrated.

LinkedIn: Professional Development.

I use LinkedIn to share my learning progress, connect with the professional community and document milestones, develop professional presence and engage with other developers.

roadmap.sh: Learning Roadmap.

roadmap.sh provides learning roadmaps for technical roles and subjects including projects per language. It is useful when understanding which topics are associated with a particular development path.

ChatGPT: Explanations and Practice Support.

With ChatGPT, I do get detailed explanation on my tutorial, what to concentrate on per language. I get study notes and also code examples after learning a language.
