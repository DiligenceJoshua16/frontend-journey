These are sample activities for the fictional project, not real events.

Ways to take part

Seed-starting session

Learn how to label seeds, prepare containers, and keep simple notes about growth.

Suitable for: Beginners

Garden tidy-up

Practise organising shared tools, clearing safe garden paths, and keeping a shared space welcoming.

Suitable for: People who enjoy teamwork

Plant observation walk

Observe leaves, growing conditions, and changes over time, then record what you notice.

Suitable for: Curious learners
