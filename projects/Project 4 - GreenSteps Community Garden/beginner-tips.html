Beginner gardening tips

You do not need to know everything before you begin. Start by observing the conditions you have.

Notice the light

Different plants have different light needs. Observe how much direct and indirect light your chosen spot receives before selecting a plant.

Water thoughtfully

Water needs depend on the plant, container, weather, and growing medium. Check the soil and the plant's needs rather than following one rule for every plant.

Keep a plant log

Short observations help you notice patterns over time.

  • Date and plant name
  • Light and weather conditions
  • Watering or other care provided
  • Changes you noticed

Quick questions

Do I need a large garden?

No. Some plants can grow in containers, but the right choice depends on the plant's needs and the space available.

What if a plant does not grow well?

Record what happened and check the plant's light, water, and space requirements. Treat it as information for your next attempt.
