What we believe

Start small

A pot, a sunny windowsill, or a shared garden bed can be a useful place to begin.

Learn together

People can share observations, ask questions, and learn from what works and what does not.

Care for the space

Good community spaces depend on respectful teamwork and clear responsibilities.
