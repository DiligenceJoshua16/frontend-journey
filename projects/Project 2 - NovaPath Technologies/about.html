Junior Frontend Development Learning Opportunity

This is a learning programme designed by NovaPath Technologies for people who are beginning their frontend development journey.

The opportunity focuses on learning and practical experience so prior professional experience is not required.

Participants would learn, practise building web pages, receive feedback, and gradually improve their development skills.

Who should apply:

  • Beginners learning frontend development.
  • Learners interested in building webpages.
  • People willing to practise, review mistakes, and improve.

Preparation Steps

In preparation for the programme, applicants should be encouraged to:

  • Review the learning paths.
  • Reflect on the frontend concepts they understand.
  • Identify relevant skills they've practiced.
  • Have a portfolio or project link available if they have one.
