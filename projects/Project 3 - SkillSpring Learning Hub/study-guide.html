A practical study guide

Use this page to turn a broad goal into small, repeatable actions.

Plan a focused session

  1. Write one goal you can finish today.
  2. Put distractions aside for a short period.
  3. Practise the concept instead of only rereading it.
  4. Write down one question or mistake to revisit.

End-of-session checklist

  • Can I explain the concept in my own words?
  • Can I use it without copying the tutorial?
  • Did I test my work at different screen sizes or conditions?
  • What will I practise next?

Common questions

Should I study for hours every day?

Not necessarily. A realistic routine that you can maintain is usually more useful than an extreme plan that quickly becomes difficult to follow.

Is it okay to look at examples?

Yes. Study examples to understand their structure, then close them and try to recreate the idea in your own way.

What should I do when I get stuck?

Describe the problem, isolate the smallest part that fails, check your assumptions, and test one change at a time.
