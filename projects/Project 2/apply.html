Who should apply:

  • Beginners learning frontend development
  • People who have studied basic HTML.

Preparation Steps

In preparation for the programme, applicants should be encouraged to:

  • Review the opportunity details.
  • Reflect on the HTML concepts they understand.
  • Identify relevant skills they've practised.
  • Prepare a short explanation of their interests.
  • Have a portfolio or project link available if they have one.

Form submissions are not processed or sent to any server. This page serves solely as a design showcase, so submitting the form does not result in a real recruitment process.
